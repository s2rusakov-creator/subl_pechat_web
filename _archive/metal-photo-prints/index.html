Why photographers choose metal prints

A metal photo print is not paper behind glass — it is the image itself, fused inside the coating of a rigid aluminium panel. Blacks read deeper, highlights carry a subtle metallic luminosity, and the surface needs no glazing, so there is nothing between the viewer and the photograph. For portrait, landscape, architectural and fine-art photographers across Dubai and the UAE, it has become the default for exhibition and client work.

We print exclusively on ChromaLuxe panels — the industry's reference substrate, with an independently tested display permanence of more than 65 years. Every file is colour-managed against a calibrated ICC profile for metal and soft-proofed before it touches the press.

Sizes, finishes and mounting

  • Sizes: from 15×15 cm desk pieces to 120×240 cm single panels; larger works are produced as multi-panel installations with precision-matched seams.
  • Finishes: gloss white for maximum vibrancy, matte white for glare-free gallery light, and brushed silver — where highlights reveal the raw metal grain.
  • Mounting: hidden float mounts as standard, so the panel appears to hover 15 mm off the wall; standoffs and flush mounts on request.

Ordering aluminium prints in Dubai

Email or WhatsApp your file with the size and finish you have in mind. We check resolution, send a colour-managed proof and a firm quote within one working day, then produce in 2–4 working days — or 48 hours on express. Delivery covers all seven Emirates, with white-glove installation available for large works.
