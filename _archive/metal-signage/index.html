Signage that is printed into the metal, not onto it

Most interior signage is vinyl or laminate on a substrate — layers that peel, scratch and dull under cleaning. Sublimated metal signage is different: your logo and brand colours are dye-infused into the coating of an aluminium panel at 199°C. There is nothing on the surface to damage, so reception logos and wayfinding stay sharp through years of daily contact.

Colour accuracy is the discipline here. We work from your vector artwork and Pantone or brand-guideline references, hold a measured tolerance of ΔE < 2, and keep the profile on file so a panel ordered next year matches the one on your wall today.

What we produce

  • Reception and lobby logos — float-mounted or standoff-fixed, gloss, matte or brushed silver.
  • Wayfinding and door signage — full suites with consistent colour and typography across every panel.
  • Branded interior panels — feature walls, values walls and campaign graphics for offices, hotels, clinics and retail.
  • Menu boards and retail displays — wipe-clean surfaces that stay presentable in F&B environments.

From site survey to installation

For fit-out contractors, interior designers and facilities teams across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the wider UAE, we handle measurement, production and white-glove installation as one scope. Standard production is 2–4 working days from approved artwork; phased delivery is available for multi-floor projects.
