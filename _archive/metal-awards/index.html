Beyond engraved acrylic

Most corporate awards are engraved acrylic or laser-etched steel — one colour, one texture, forgettable by the following quarter. Sublimated metal awards carry full-colour artwork inside the panel itself: brand gradients, photography, portraits of the recipient's work — anything you can design, rendered with the depth and permanence of dye fused into aluminium.

Type stays engrave-sharp because it is printed at high resolution and infused, not cut. Names, dates and citations remain crisp at small sizes, and the surface never chips or fades.

Formats and mounting

  • Desk awards — panels on weighted metal or timber bases, from 15×15 cm.
  • Wall plaques — float-mounted recognition walls, employee-of-the-quarter series, donor walls.
  • Milestone pieces — retirements, closings, launches; single pieces receive the same colour management as production runs.
  • Series & programmes — repeat quarterly or annual awards, with your template kept on file for exact consistency.

Built for event deadlines

Award ceremonies do not move, so we plan backwards from your event date. Standard production is 2–4 working days from approved artwork; 48-hour express covers late confirmations, and delivery reaches venues across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and all Emirates. For runs above fifty pieces we produce a physical pre-production sample for sign-off.
